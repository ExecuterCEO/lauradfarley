How much home, or how much income?

Use one calculator. Switch the question. Taxes, HOA dues, and Mello-Roos are different for each home, so those boxes start empty.

Down payment

Example only. Freddie Mac’s average 30-year fixed rate was 7.24% on October 29, 2026, for excellent credit and 20% down. It is not your rate.

If you enter both, the dollar bill is used. Temecula is not one tax rate. Mello-Roos and other assessments sit in the boxes below, not inside this percent.

If the down payment is under 20%, a lender may add mortgage insurance. Enter that amount if you have it. This box does not guess it. At 100% down, mortgage insurance is left out.

100% is a cash purchase. The result still includes the income needed to carry taxes, insurance, HOA, and other dues, with no loan payment.

28% and 36% are planning ratios, not a lender’s rule for you. VA and FHA loans can differ. Change these if a lender gave you different numbers.

Enter the figures, then press Calculate.

This is a planning estimate. It is not a loan approval, a tax opinion, or a promise of what you will qualify for. A lender, the tax bill, and the HOA documents control the real numbers.
