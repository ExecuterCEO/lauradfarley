Practice experience

Built for more than a rising market

Licensed since 2005, I have worked through more than one full cycle. The work below is part of how I still advise sellers and buyers when the path is not a simple retail sale.

Short-sale negotiation

From the 2008 downturn through recovery into 2012, a large part of my practice was short-sale negotiation: gathering hardship and financial packages, working with lenders and mortgage insurers, and keeping sellers informed while timelines stretched. That period still matters. If values soften or equity is thin, a listing is not always a conventional sale. Having negotiated short sales when the market was under real stress means I can recognize when a file needs lender cooperation, how to set expectations on timing, and how to protect the homeowner’s next step instead of forcing a path that will not close.

VA and FHA buyers

I have represented buyers using VA and FHA financing. Those loans are powerful, and they are not identical to conventional purchase money. Appraisals, property condition, condo or HOA eligibility, and seller credits all have to line up, or the contract stalls. I help veterans, active-duty buyers, and FHA purchasers read the offer through the lender’s lens, and I help sellers understand what those terms mean before they accept so we are not surprised in escrow.

1031 exchanges, including multi-state

I have worked 1031 exchange transactions, including exchanges that crossed state lines. A 1031 is a tax-timed sale and purchase, not a casual “sell this, buy that.” Identification windows, qualified intermediaries, replacement-property rules, and coordination with CPAs and exchange companies have to stay on calendar. Multi-state work adds title, disclosure, and closing customs that differ from California. I do not give tax advice. I do keep the real estate side sequenced so the exchange has a chance to perform the way your tax counsel designed it. Read the full notes: 1031 exchanges, including multi-state.

FAQ

Common questions

How much is my Temecula home worth? +

A useful answer starts with recent comparable sales, current competition, condition, and location nuances. I provide a complimentary market valuation and walk you through the reasoning behind the number.

Is now a good time to sell? +

It depends on your equity position, timeline, local inventory, and what you need next. Sometimes waiting or prepping first is the smarter path. I’ll give you a direct read for your situation.

Do I need to make repairs before listing? +

Not always. We’ll separate high-ROI fixes from nice-to-haves so you invest where it counts. Presentation and pricing often matter more than a full remodel.

Can I buy before I sell? +

Often yes, with the right structure: contingencies, bridge options, or sequenced timing. We’ll map the path that fits your risk tolerance and financing.

Do you work with buyers as well as sellers? +

Yes. I list and sell residential real estate — representing sellers bringing a home to market and buyers searching for their next home. You’ll work directly with your agent either way.

What makes you different from other Realtors? +

You work directly with your agent, a clear focus on residential listing and selling, 21+ years as a licensed REALTOR®, and an MBA-informed approach to pricing and negotiation built around strategy and communication.

Do you help with estates or more complex sales? +

Yes. Sensitive timelines, multiple decision-makers, and “what do we do first?” situations are welcome. We’ll start with a calm plan and next steps.

Have you handled short sales? +

Yes. From 2008 through recovery into 2012, short-sale negotiation was a substantial part of my practice. If equity is thin or the cycle turns, that lender-side experience still applies.

Do you work with VA or FHA buyers? +

Yes. I have represented VA and FHA purchasers and help sellers understand those loan terms before they accept an offer.

Can you help with a 1031 exchange? +

Yes, including multi-state exchanges. I coordinate the real estate timeline with your qualified intermediary and tax counsel. I do not give tax advice. More detail: 1031 exchange notes on this site.
